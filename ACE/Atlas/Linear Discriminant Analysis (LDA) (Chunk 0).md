The goal is to find the optimal model complexity that minimizes both. Key Concepts: Bias: Error from erroneous assumptions, leading to underfitting (e.g., linear model for non-linear data). Variance: Error from sensitivity to small fluctuations in the training set, leading to overfitting. High Bias, Low Variance: Model is too simple, misses patterns (underfit). Low Bias, High Variance: Model is too complex, captures noise (overfit). Optimal Balance: Low bias and low variance. Strategies to Manage the Tradeoff: Regularization: Adds penalties for complexity (e.g., L1/L2). Ensemble Methods: Using techniques like Random Forests (reduces variance) or Boosting (reduces bias). The goal is to select a model that strikes the right balance to minimize the total error, ensuring good performance on both training and test data.
