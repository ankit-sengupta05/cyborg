A biological neuron has three main parts : 1.Dendrites – These are branches that receive input signals from other neurons . 2.Cell Body (Soma) – It processes the input signals received from dendrites . 3.Axon – This is the output part that transmits the processed signal to the next neuron . In the human brain, neurons pass electrical and chemical signals between each other, forming a neural network that allows us to think, learn, and process information . •The perceptron learns through supervised learning by adjusting its weights to minimize classification error.
